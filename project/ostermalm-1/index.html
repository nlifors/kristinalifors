Östermalm Ten-Room Apartment

Situated in one of the most beautiful neighborhoods in central Stockholm, this grand 10 room apartment was fully renovated and decorated with a close collaboration with the client. An early request for the direction of the design was ”rather 1980 than 1890”.

We set out to create an elegant and classic space, with beautiful textiles and a mix of old and new pieces, such as the brass Rams head desk holding center stage in the husbands study.
