Tegnerlunden

This turn of the century apartment had all the beautiful features one looks for in an apartment in Stockholm. Large rooms with generous ceiling height, beautiful window casings and door frames, all original architectural features, as well as a south facing balcony on the top floor of the building.

Four grand windows overlooking Tegnerlunden, a lush and quiet park nestled in amongst magnificent buildings, make it even the better. A total makeover was done adding a custom build kitchen and bathroom, a cozy fireplace with custom made marble surround added to the living room and a tiny bedroom was transformed into a walk in closet.

As this was my second time working with this client a lot of the previously purchased furniture was used as well as adding new custom rugs and curtains to fit the new space.
