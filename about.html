The Studio

Kristina Lifors

A boutique residential interior design practice based in Stockholm — born of a decade in New York, anchored in the quiet rigour of Scandinavian design.

Biography

A Swedish eye, an American education.

Kristina Lifors' love of interiors began as a child, watching her parents shape their home in Naples, Florida through the 1990s. She studied Marketing at the University of Rhode Island before following that early instinct back to design.

In New York she joined the renowned Brian J. McCarthy Inc., rising from assistant designer to working alongside Brian on landmark residential projects — Manhattan apartments, Hamptons summer estates and a private residence in Bel Air. Nine years later she returned to Stockholm and opened her own studio.

Today, Kristina Lifors Interior Design AB is a small boutique practice working on turn-key residential projects across Sweden and abroad — from full renovation to the final placement of a single, considered object.

“Good rooms aren't designed all at once — they're slowly assembled, with patience and a point of view.”
Kristina Lifors
Approach

Quiet, layered, entirely yours.

Each project begins with conversation — understanding how a family lives, eats, reads, entertains and rests. From there, plans are reshaped, joinery drawn, palettes assembled and pieces sourced from antique dealers, craftspeople and ateliers we have come to trust over many years.

The studio is deliberately small. Kristina is personally involved in every project from first sketch to the day the last lamp is plugged in.
