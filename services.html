What we do

Services

A full-service residential design studio. We work on everything from gut renovations to single-room decoration — collaborating closely with architects, craftspeople and clients on a small handful of projects each year.

01

Concept & Space Planning

Every project begins with a careful understanding of how a home is lived in. We re-draw plans, propose new openings, and shape rooms to suit the way our clients actually move through their day.

02

Full Renovation Management

Working alongside architects, contractors and tradespeople, we manage the build from first demolition to handover — surfaces, fittings, joinery, lighting plans, electrical and plumbing detail.

03

Bespoke Joinery & Millwork

Kitchens, libraries, wardrobes, panelling and built-in seating, all drawn from scratch and produced by Swedish cabinetmakers we have worked with for years.

04

Materials, Colour & Textiles

Complete palettes — paint, stone, timber, wallpaper, fabrics and rugs — assembled to feel collected rather than coordinated, and selected to wear beautifully over many years.

05

Furniture & Lighting

We source from antique dealers, auctions, independent ateliers and a careful set of contemporary makers. Custom upholstery and bespoke lighting are commissioned where the right piece doesn't yet exist.

06

Decoration & Final Styling

Books, art, ceramics, linens, glassware, flowers — the slower, quieter layer that gives a finished room its life. We install, we style, and we leave behind a home that is ready to be lived in.

“Begin with a conversation. Every project is shaped, from the very first sketch, around the people who will live there.”
